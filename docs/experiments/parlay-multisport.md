# Cross-game correlated parlays: NBA, NHL, MLB, soccer

Same hypothesis and machinery as `parlay-interaction-effects.md` (NFL), applied to daily sports. Anchor game H vs A; legs are H's next game and A's next game against different opponents, priced independently by the book. Explaining-away predicts a positive correlation of the two margin residuals and a negative correlation of the two total residuals. NHL and MLB use the closing moneyline as the primary market: the market-implied margin is `sd * Phi^-1(p_home)` with `p_home` vig-free and `sd` fitted by OLS; parlays on those legs are priced at the actual closing decimals.

Hurdle: 2-leg spread/total parlay at -110 needs phi > 0.098 (same-sign rate 54.9%). Generated by `scripts/backtest_parlay_multisport.py`.
